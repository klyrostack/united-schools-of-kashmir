About Us

United Schools of Kashmir (USK) is a digital platform designed to connect and empower schools across Kashmir through technology and accessible education services. Our vision is to create a unified digital space where schools, students, parents, teachers, and administrators can access important academic information, notices, examinations, results, documents, and school services in one place. USK aims to support schools in their journey toward digital transformation by making information more organized, accessible, and efficient. By bringing schools and their communities closer through technology, we strive to contribute to a more connected, transparent, and digitally empowered education system across Kashmir.
